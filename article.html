How to Share Your Testimony: Tips, Examples, and Themes

Learn to tell the story of how God has impacted your life.

Your testimony is one of the most powerful tools you have. It's simply the story of your life before Christ, how you met him, and the difference he has made since. No one can argue with your experience.

Keep it simple: before, how, after

A clear testimony has three parts. What was your life like before you followed Jesus? How did you come to trust him? And what has changed since? Aim for three to five minutes.

Make it relatable to students

Use everyday language, avoid Christian jargon, and be honest about the hard parts. Students connect with authenticity far more than polish.

"They triumphed over him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony." — Revelation 12:11

Practice out loud

Write it down, then say it to a friend. The more comfortable you are telling your story, the more naturally it will come up in real conversations on campus.
