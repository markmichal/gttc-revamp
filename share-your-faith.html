Share Your Faith

Spiritual conversations don't have to be intimidating. Here's how to take initiative, listen well, and point students to Jesus.

Body

Sharing your faith is simply telling someone about the most important relationship in your life. It starts with genuine care for the student in front of you and a willingness to take the first step in a conversation.

Start with a conversation, not a presentation

The best spiritual conversations grow out of real relationships. Ask questions, listen more than you talk, and look for natural openings to share what God has done in your life.

"When in doubt, go to the campus. That's where the students are, and that's where God is already at work."
