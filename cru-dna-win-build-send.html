Cru DNA: Win, Build, Send

Home / Learn / Understand Cru

▶ Cru Promo Video - 3 min — watch on YouTube

Every ministry has its own way of reaching students. At Cru, we focus on a simple but powerful process to help students come to know Jesus, grow in their faith, and begin sharing it with others. We call it: Win. Build. Send. These three words shape everything we do.

Win

We begin by showing up in students’ lives, meeting them where they are, listening well, and starting meaningful conversations. We call this sowing. It’s all about building relationships and creating space for spiritual curiosity to grow.

When the time is right, we move into winning, which means clearly sharing the gospel and inviting students to respond to Jesus. This can happen one-on-one, in small groups, or during a larger outreach event. Our heart is to help every student understand who Jesus is and how to begin a relationship with Him.

“Our heart is to help every student understand who Jesus is and how to begin a relationship with Him.”

Build

After a student decides to follow Christ, we want to help them get rooted in their new faith. This starts with follow-up and basic growth: meeting personally, helping them understand the basics of walking with God, and answering the questions they’re asking.

From there, we move into discipleship and training. As students grow in their relationship with Jesus, we help them develop spiritual habits and equip them to lead and serve others. We don’t just invite students into ministry, we train them to lead it.

Send

Every follower of Jesus has influence, and we want students to see themselves as missionaries right where they are. This is what we mean by influencing others. We challenge and train students to share their faith and help others grow, whether that’s on their own campus, in another part of the city, or across the globe.

Through Cru conferences, local outreach, and global missions, we help students and leaders discover how God can use them to make a difference in the lives of others. Reaching a school starts with one student saying yes to being sent.

Cru is here to walk with you as you walk toward students. Whether you’re a college student, a high school leader, or a parent with a heart for teens, you can be part of a movement that changes campuses and changes lives. If you care about reaching high school students, we’ll help you take the next step.

In Cru, we

• Build real friendships with students, earning trust through presence, not pressure

• Share Christ clearly and respectfully at the first good opportunity

• Create safe, welcoming spaces like small groups where students can process life, faith, and questions

• Invite students to lead so they don’t just join the mission, they own it

• Train students to multiply through personal conversations, group outreaches, and weekly or monthly gatherings

• Intentionally reach out to every ethnic group on campus so every student knows they belong

• Meet students where they are, brokenness and all, and walk with them as they take steps toward Jesus
