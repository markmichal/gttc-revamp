Cru’s Coaching Center is Here to Help!

▶ Cru's High School Ministry — watch on YouTube

You might be a teacher, youth pastor, college student, or just a concerned adult who has a vision to reach your local school. But where do you start?

Cru wants to help you as you seek to reach your school for Christ. We have professional coaches standing by to pray for you, hear your vision, and coach you as you take your first steps.

Our main goals are to help you do the following:

  • WIN students to Jesus
  • BUILD them up in their faith
  • SEND them back to their campus, equipped with the knowledge and training to reach others for Christ

Reaching Your Local Campus

The High School Ministry of Cru has resources to help you reach students on your local campus one-on-one and also in their natural groups/cliques. As you spend time on campus reaching students, you will also meet their teachers and coaches. That opens up the opportunity to offer classroom talks or motivational talks to different clubs and teams they oversee. We can help you learn how to speak to students and we even have a number of great talk outlines you can use!

Cru wants to help you as you seek to reach your school for Christ.

Conferences

Another opportunity the High School Ministry of Cru offers is our conferences. We host two conferences a year, one during the winter, and another during summer break in June. These conferences can range in size from about 200 to over 500 students. At these conferences, students hear from speakers and other youth who share their faith on campus. Students who attend our conferences will learn how to share their faith. If the student already knows how to share their faith, we encourage them to pray for the natural groups in which they are involved and ask how God can use them to reach their friends.

Mission Trips

Lastly, we want to help students reach the world. We offer short-term mission trips all around the globe. During these trips, students go out and share the love of Jesus with teenagers from other countries as they learn about each other’s cultures.

Has God laid it on your heart to reach out to your local school? We would love to help!

Next Step

Find a personal ministry coach by calling 1-877-GO-CAMPUS or visiting us at our Cru website.
