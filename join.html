3 WAYS TO

JOIN US

As a Volunteer

Teenagers thrive in an atmosphere of love and acceptance.  Cru is a group of caring, trusted adults and college students who enter into students’ lives and love them with the love of Jesus.  Our field volunteers meet the students where they are and help them grow to their potential.

And if working directly with students isn’t for you, but you still want to make a difference, we are always looking for teacher sponsors, social media helpers, event volunteers, and people to open their homes for Cru to meet.

To Apply…

Talk to your local Cru leader about volunteering and they will send you an application link.

As an Intern

Considering taking a year or two to invest in teenagers? We invite you to intern to grow in discipleship, evangelism, and leadership skills. Whether you decide to join staff after your internship or take this as an opportunity to grow, you can have an eternal impact as you seek God’s direction.

If you have any questions about applying for the Cru High School Internship, please email Nicole Staggs.

As a Staff

If you love working with teenagers and want to invest in them full-time or part-time, consider joining staff with us! Learn to build spiritual movements of evangelism and engage in Christ-centered discipleship with students on campuses in your neighborhood and all over the world.

OTHER WAY TO JOIN US

Want to try something for a year that will build your ministry and professional skills while impacting young people for eternity? Check out these options.

The Apprentice Program

Learn to reach high school students from veteran youth workers for 5-10 hours per week and get paid to do it! Available in select cities. Contact Shawn Faulkner for more details.

Gap Year

If you love investing in college freshmen, we are looking for leaders (college students and adults) to help lead our Gap Year program each year. Learn more about Gap Year here.
